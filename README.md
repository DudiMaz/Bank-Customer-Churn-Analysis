
# Bank Customer Churn Analysis 🏦

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes bank customer churn using Python, with the goal of understanding customer behavior, identifying patterns associated with churn, and exploring opportunities to improve customer retention.

The analysis covers data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, visualization, and business-oriented questions.

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Faker
- Excel

## 🧹 Data Preparation

The data preparation process included:

- Cleaning column names and converting financial values into numeric formats.
- Converting date columns into datetime format.
- Checking duplicate records and categorical values.
- Validating customer churn status and churn dates.
- Creating age and churn-status columns.
- Generating synthetic Botswana regions and addresses for the analysis.

## 📊 Business Questions

### 1. Overall Customer Churn
What percentage of customers left the bank, and what reasons did they report?

### 2. Account Balance and Churn
How does account balance differ between churned and retained customers?

### 3. Customer Complaints
How does churn rate change as the number of complaints increases, and how does account balance relate to complaints?

### 4. Number of Banking Products
Does the number of products held by a customer relate to churn risk?

### 5. Credit Score
How does credit score differ between churned and retained customers?

### 6. High-Risk Customer Profiles
Which combinations of complaints, products, account balance, and credit score can help identify potentially high-risk customers?

## 🔍 Key Findings

- The analysis reported an overall customer churn rate of approximately 12.2%.
- Customer complaints showed a strong increasing pattern alongside churn rates.
- Product holdings, account balances, and credit scores were examined as potential churn indicators.
- Customers with high complaint counts, fewer products, lower balances, and lower credit scores were selected for further risk investigation.
- Service-related issues were highlighted as an actionable area for customer retention efforts.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Prioritize investigation of customers with repeated complaints.
- Explore retention strategies for customers holding fewer banking products.
- Use combinations of customer characteristics to identify groups for proactive follow-up.
- Improve service quality as a potential way to address avoidable churn.
